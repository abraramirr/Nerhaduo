About Nerhadou International

Nerhadou International is a leading Egyptian pharmaceutical and nutraceutical company established in 1996 by Dr. Mohsen Shalaby , former Vice President of Bristol-Myers Squibb for Egypt, the Middle East, and Africa. Since its foundation, the company has been dedicated to improving health and enhancing quality of life through innovative, high-quality healthcare solutions

With state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in 6th of October City, Egypt, Nerhadou produces a wide range of pharmaceutical products, nutraceuticals, cosmetics, and healthcare solutions that meet international quality standards. The company is recognized for its commitment to research, innovation, and excellence in manufacturing.

As a pioneer in the Middle East, Nerhadou has introduced advanced healthcare technologies , including Orally Dissolvable Film (ODF) technology , providing patients with innovative and convenient treatment options. The company continues to expand its presence in regional and international markets while maintaining its mission of delivering safe , effective, and affordable healthcare products.

Our Mission

To improve health and quality of life by providing innovative, safe, and high-quality pharmaceutical and nutraceutical products that meet the needs of patients and healthcare professionals.

Our Vision

To be a trusted global leader in pharmaceutical and nutraceutical innovation, recognized for excellence, quality, and a commitment to advancing healthcare worldwide.
