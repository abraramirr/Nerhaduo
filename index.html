About Our Company

Nerhadou International is a premier Egyptian pharmaceutical and nutraceutical company founded in 1996 by Dr. Mohsen Shalaby, a former Bristol-Myers Squibb VP. Headquartered in 6th of October City, it is one of Egypt's top 53 leading pharmaceutical corporates and a pioneer in amino-acid chelated minerals.The company is globally recognized and highly active both locally and internationally:Manufacturing Facility: Operates a state-of-the-art, fully automated plant with a capacity of 430 million capsules per year. It complies with FDA, EMEA, and GCC GMP standards. Innovative Products: Known for popular brands like Zinctron, Octatron, Ferrotron, and Methyltechno. They are also a regional pioneer in manufacturing Oral Dispersible Films (ODF) for rapid drug delivery. Global Reach: Exports to over 20 countries across the Middle East, Africa, Russia, and the Caucasus.
